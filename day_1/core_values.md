COE Day 1: 

Incubyte core values: 

1. Quality with Pragmatism 

Aim for high quality but stay practical.  

Deliver value, avoid over-engineering.  

2. Extreme Ownership 

Take full responsibility for work and outcomes.  

Focus on team success and reduce WIP.  

3. Proactive Collaboration 

Communicate clearly.  

Help teammates, share knowledge, and solve problems together.  

4. Pursuit of Mastery 

Continuously learn and improve skills.  

Take ownership of personal growth.  

5. Feedback Culture 

Give and receive honest feedback.  

Use feedback for improvement.  

6. Ecstatic Customers 

Focus on customer success and real value delivery.  

Understand the user’s needs and context.
