Engineer

Core idea
A product-minded engineer is not just focused on writing code — they deeply care about the product, users, and business outcomes. They try to understand why something is being built, not just how.

Why it matters
These engineers have higher impact because they influence both product decisions and implementation.
They often become key contributors or leaders in teams.
Modern teams (especially startups) value this mindset more than just coding ability.

Key traits (simplified)
1. Proactive with ideas: Don’t just follow specs — suggest better solutions.
2. Understand business + users: Know how the product makes money and how users behave.
3. Curious about “why”: Constantly question decisions to understand purpose.
4. Strong collaboration: Work closely with product managers, designers, etc.
5. Balance product vs engineering trade-offs: Think about effort and user impact together.
6. Pragmatic about edge cases: Focus on what matters most (not perfection).
7. Validate early and often: Get feedback before and after shipping features
